## The Central Claim

"The resurrection of Jesus is either the most well-documented event in ancient history, or it is a elaborate deception. There is no third option."

## Evidence for the Resurrection

### 1. The Empty Tomb
- Women discovered the tomb first (unlikely to be fabricated)
- Jewish and Roman sources acknowledge the tomb was empty

### 2. The Appearances
- Multiple appearances to different groups
- Appearances in Galilee and Jerusalem
- Over 500 witnesses at once (1 Corinthians 15:6)

### 3. The Transformation
- disciples went from fearful to bold
- Willing to die for their claim

### 4. Early Attestation
- Creed in 1 Corinthians 15:3-8 dates to within years of the event

### 5. Hostile Sources
- Even Tacitus and Josephus mention Jesus

## Alternative Theories

- Swoon Theory
- Hallucination Theory
- Wrong Tomb Theory
- Disciples stole the body
