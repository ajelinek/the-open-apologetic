## Evidence of Design

The female reproductive system shows remarkable evidence of intelligent design—not random chance.

## The Bladder Connection

During pregnancy, the uterus expands and puts pressure on the bladder. This causes pregnant women to need to urinate more frequently, often waking up multiple times during the night.

### The Training Effect

This is not an accident or flaw—it's a design feature:
- The mother develops the habit of waking every couple of hours during pregnancy
- When the baby arrives, she's already conditioned to wake regularly
- This prepares her for nighttime feedings

This is just one example of the intricate design built into the female body to enable mothers to care for their newborns.
