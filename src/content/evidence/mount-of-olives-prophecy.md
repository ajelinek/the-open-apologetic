## The Prophecy

Zechariah 14:4 states: "On that day his feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem, and the Mount of Olives will be split in two..."

This is a prophecy about the return of Jesus Christ.

## The Discovery

In 1964, Holiday Inn (now InterContinental Hotels Group) planned to build a hotel on the Mount of Olives. They commissioned a geological survey to assess the feasibility of construction.

### What the Survey Found

The engineers discovered a major geological fault line running east-to-west directly through the Mount of Olives. This fault line is actively causing the mountain to slowly split apart.

### The Project Was Abandoned

Because of this discovery, the hotel construction was deemed too dangerous and the project was cancelled.

## The Significance

- The Bible, written ~2,500 years ago, prophesied the mountain would split
- Modern science, 2,500 years later, confirmed a fault line exists that is literally splitting the mountain
- This is not coincidence—this is prophetic confirmation

## Archaeological Evidence

Recent excavations have also found evidence of a major earthquake in Jerusalem during the 8th century BC, mentioned by the prophet Amos. This earthquake aligns with end-times prophetic literature.
