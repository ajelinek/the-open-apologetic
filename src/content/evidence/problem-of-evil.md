## The Classic Problem

This is perhaps the most difficult objection to Christianity:
1. God is all-powerful (omnipotent)
2. God is all-loving (omnibenevolent)
3. Evil and suffering exist

How can all three be true?

## Responses to Consider

### Free Will
God gave humans free will. Love requires the choice to love. Without free will, we would be robots, not beings capable of genuine love.

### Greater Good
Some suffering produces greater goods that would not otherwise exist—courage, compassion, deeper character.

### The Existence of Evil
Evil is not a "thing" but a absence of good, like darkness is the absence of light.

### The Cross
God did not remain distant from suffering—He entered into it through Jesus Christ.
