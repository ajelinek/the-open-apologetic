Here's something that distinguishes the Bible from every other religious text: the Bible's heroes are deeply flawed. Moses was a murderer. David was an adulterer and murderer. Peter denied Jesus three times. Paul persecuted Christians.

This is incredibly strange—if humans were fabricating these stories, why would they include such embarrassing details?

## What Makes This Significant

Compare this to other religious texts:
- **The Quran:** Describes Muhammad in idealized terms
- **The Book of Mormon:** Describes Joseph Smith without serious flaws
- **Buddhist texts:** Present the Buddha as perfectly enlightened from the start

Only the Bible shows its heroes as real people with real struggles.

## The Faithful Failures

- **Abraham:** Lied about his wife being his sister (twice)
- **Moses:** Murdered an Egyptian, struck the rock instead of speaking to it
- **David:** Committed adultery with Bathsheba, arranged for her husband's death
- **Peter:** Denied knowing Jesus three times
- **All the apostles:** Abandoned Jesus in his hour of need

:::quote
"The Bible doesn't present heroes—it presents humanity. And that's exactly what makes it divine."
:::

## The Catholic Connection

The Catholic Church teaches that Scripture is divinely inspired but written by human authors in their own contexts and limitations. The flaws of Biblical heroes actually confirm the Bible's authenticity—it reads like real history from real people, not like a sanitized legend.
