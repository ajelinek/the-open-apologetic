In 1947, a Bedouin shepherd discovered ancient manuscripts in caves near the Dead Sea. These would become known as the Dead Sea Scrolls—some of the oldest biblical manuscripts ever found.

## What the Scrolls Reveal

- **Dated to:** 125 BC - 68 AD
- **Location:** Qumran caves near the Dead Sea
- **Scrolls found:** Over 900 manuscripts
- **Books represented:** Every book of the Old Testament except Esther

The significance is enormous. These scrolls predate the previously oldest Hebrew manuscripts by over 1,000 years.

## Textual Accuracy

When scholars compared the Dead Sea Scrolls to later manuscripts (like the Masoretic Text from 900 AD), they found remarkable agreement:

- **98-99% identical** in terms of textual content
- The minor differences are spelling variations and minor stylistic changes
- No doctrinal changes between the scrolls and later texts

This proves that the Old Testament text we have today is virtually identical to what was written 1,000 years earlier.

:::info
The Dead Sea Scrolls provide powerful confirmation that the Hebrew Bible has been faithfully preserved over millennia.
:::

## The Catholic Connection

The Catholic Church has always affirmed the divine inspiration and reliability of Scripture. The Dead Sea Scrolls provide independent archaeological confirmation of what the Church has taught: God's Word has been faithfully transmitted through the ages.
