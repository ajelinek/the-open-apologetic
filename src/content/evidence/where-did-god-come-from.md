## The Question

This is perhaps the most frequently asked question about faith. If everything needs a cause, doesn't God need one too?

## The Answer

The key insight is understanding the difference between:
- **Contingent beings** - things that exist but could have not existed, things that depend on something else for their existence
- **Necessary being** - a being that must exist, that contains the reason for its own existence within itself
